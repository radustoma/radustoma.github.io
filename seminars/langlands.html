Learning seminar on Eisenstein series and the genesis of the Langlands program

The goal of this groupe de travail is to understand the general theory of Eisenstein series, in particular for the group GL(n). Amongst other applications, we are motivated by understanding how computing constant terms of Eisenstein series led Langlands to his conjectures, particularly functoriality.
